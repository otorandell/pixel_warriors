## Next Session — Combat Depth Overhaul

Combat currently feels like "button clicking" — most turns the optimal play is just "pick highest damage ability." Root causes identified:

### Problem Analysis
1. **Enemy AI is purely random** — picks random ability, random aggro-weighted target. No strategy.
2. **Most abilities are damage variants** — "pick highest multiplier" is always correct.
3. **No resource pressure** — Energy/Mana regen passively (Survivalist +2EN/turn, Inner Light +2MP/turn), shields absorb damage. You never run out of anything.
4. **No multi-turn planning** — each turn is independent. Setup abilities exist (Mark, Vanish, Envenom) but payoffs are immediate and optional.
5. **Positioning is static** — enemies don't move, Close vs Reach rarely matters, grid is 2x4.
6. **Action point system is trivial** — 1L+1S is a loose constraint. Only Earthquake costs both.

### Proposed Changes (Priority Order)

**1. Enemy AI Overhaul (highest impact)**
Replace random ability selection with priority-based behavior:
- Healers: prioritize healing when allies < 50% HP
- Tanks: taunt when backline allies are targeted
- Assassins: target lowest HP enemy, prefer single-target high-damage
- Mages: AoE when 3+ targets, single-target otherwise
- Support: buff/heal before attacking
- All enemies: finish off low-HP targets instead of spreading damage

**2. Enemy Threat Abilities**
Add abilities that force player reactions:
- **Charge-up attacks:** Enemy telegraphs a big hit next turn (visible status). Player MUST stun, kill, or shield.
- **Enrage on ally death:** Surviving enemies get stronger when an ally dies. Punishes "kill weakest first" strategy.
- **Action point drain:** Enemy ability that removes a short action from a player character.
- **Team buffs:** Enemy casters buff ally damage/defense. Creates priority targets.
- **Position displacement:** Enemy abilities that push player characters between front/backline.
- **Revive/summon:** Some enemies can bring back dead allies or summon adds mid-fight.

**3. Ability Synergies & Combos**
Make effects interact for bonus results:
- Wet (from water spells) + Lightning = bonus damage + stun
- Bleed + physical hit = bonus damage (hemorrhage)
- Marked targets take crit damage from any source
- Burn + Poison = explosion (consumes both, AoE damage)
- Frozen Tomb expiring = shatter damage to nearby enemies
- Elemental combos: Fire→Water = Steam (AoE debuff), Water→Fire = evaporate shield

**4. Resource Scarcity**
- Reduce or remove passive regen passives from base kits (make them earn-only via level-up)
- Increase costs for powerful abilities (Earthquake 6EN, Chain Lightning 7MP)
- Add abilities that drain enemy resources (Mana Burn, Energy Sap)
- Shields should have durability limits or expiration timers

**5. Positioning Depth**
- Give enemies movement abilities (charge to backline, swap positions)
- Make frontline/backline matter more: backline takes reduced AoE, frontline blocks backline targeting
- Add more Close-only powerful abilities to make frontline risky but rewarding

### Implementation Order
1. Enemy AI behavior system (EnemyAI.cs rewrite with priority tables)
2. New enemy abilities (charge-up, enrage, buffs) across all 4 acts
3. Status effect interactions (combo system)
4. Ability cost rebalance
5. Positioning improvements
